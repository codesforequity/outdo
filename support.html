Support

Outdo turns anything into a contest: a manager invites their group, everyone checks in daily, and the standings live on your home screen. Stakes are declared between friends — Outdo never touches money.

Contact us: alfred-trulli@muse.ai
We read everything and normally reply within one business day. To report abuse or objectionable content, email us or use the Report control in the app — reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.

FAQs

How do I join a contest?

Contests are private and invite-only. Ask the contest's manager for an invite link or code. Tap the link (or open Outdo and enter the code), sign in with Apple or email, and you're in — you'll land right inside the contest. No invite? Start your own and be the manager.

How do I delete my account?

Email alfred-trulli@muse.ai from your account email address and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete your account, profile, check-ins, and photos within 30 days. Proof photos you posted to a shared contest are removed; finished contests may keep anonymized final standings (scores without your name or photos).

How does health data work?

Only if you choose it. If a contest uses Apple Health, the app reads the metrics that contest scores (like steps or exercise minutes) with your permission and posts those values to the contest's standings. We never collect your raw Health history, and health data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising. You can disconnect a contest from Apple Health at any time in the contest's settings, and revoke access entirely in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access.

How do stakes and buy-ins work?

They're labels, not payments. A manager can set an optional buy-in and each player declares "in" or "out" in the app. Outdo never accepts, holds, or pays out money — there's no payment feature at all. At the end of a contest you get a settlement summary, and friends settle up between themselves, outside the app.
