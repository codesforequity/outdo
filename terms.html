What Outdo is

Outdo is an app for friendly competitions between people who know each other. A manager creates a contest — 100 push-ups a day, most steps this week, a no-sugar streak — invites their group, and everyone checks in by hand, with a photo, or through Apple Health. Standings show in the app and in a home-screen widget. Contests are private: only invited members can see a contest and its contents.
