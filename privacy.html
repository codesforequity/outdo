Pact Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Pact ("we," "us") is an app for friendly competitions between people who know each other. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect what a scoreboard needs, we show it to your contest group, and we never sell it.

What we collect

Account information. When you create an account (Sign in with Apple or email), we receive your name and email address. If you use Sign in with Apple with "Hide My Email," we only receive the private relay address Apple gives us.

Profile information. Your display name and, if you add one, a profile photo.

Contest content. The contests you create or join, your check-in values (for example, a push-up count or a step total), the dates of your check-ins, and any proof photos you choose to upload. Contest names are written by users.

Health data. If you connect Apple Health to a contest, the app reads the HealthKit metrics that contest uses (such as steps, walking/running distance, or exercise minutes) and posts the resulting values to that contest's standings. We collect the values you post to a shared contest — not your raw HealthKit history.

Basic technical data. Crash and diagnostic information provided by Apple if you have opted in to sharing it with developers.

How we use it

We use this data only to run the app: to show standings to the members of your contests, to send the notifications you enable, to operate your account, and to keep the service working. HealthKit data is used only to score the contests you joined.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share HealthKit data with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms, and HealthKit data is never used for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not collect payment information. Pact does not process payments of any kind; any stakes between players are declared in the app and settled entirely outside it.

Who can see what

Your display name, profile photo, check-in values, and proof photos are visible to the members of the contests you join — that visibility is the product. They are not public and are not visible to other Pact users outside your contests. Our backend provider (Supabase) stores the data on our behalf; it does not use it for its own purposes.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and contest data while your account is active. You can delete your account and your data at any time: email alfred-trulli@muse.ai from your account email address and we will delete your account, profile, check-ins, and photos within 30 days. Proof photos you posted to a shared contest are removed; final standings for a finished contest may be retained in anonymized form (scores without your name or photos).

Children

Pact is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the date above. Material changes will be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: alfred-trulli@muse.ai
